## Floréa Diamond Heritage

Visual identity for Floréa, a premium diamond jewelry brand embodying timeless elegance and sophisticated luxury. The project pairs refined custom typography with a warm, editorial color palette — soft greys, terracotta, deep rust, and a single muted teal accent. The logo mark reflects the delicate brilliance of diamonds, blending classic heritage with modern minimalism. The identity extends cohesively across digital platforms, luxury packaging, and print collateral.
