## Architectural Warmth

Brand identity for Nordhive — a real estate platform built around calmer, more considered property discovery. The identity combines warm earthy tones, refined materials, and a geometric visual language inspired by modern architecture. Every element is designed to feel cohesive, premium, and quietly confident.
