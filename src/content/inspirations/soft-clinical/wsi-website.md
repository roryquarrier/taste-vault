## WSI Systems Clarity

Website design for WSI, a company operating in commercial laundry systems, chemistry, and performance optimization. The challenge was making complex systems feel simple without dumbing anything down. The solution focuses on structure, clarity, and rhythm — stripping back where needed and building a scalable system across dense technical content. A restrained cool palette of teals, blues, and sage greens runs throughout on a dark ground.
