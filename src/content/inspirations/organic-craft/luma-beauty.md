## LUMÉA Beauty Warmth

A beauty and skincare e-commerce concept designed to make discovering everyday essentials feel simple, premium, and enjoyable. The visual direction combines expressive typography, soft product imagery, and a warm palette of corals, dusty rose, and terracotta on a near-white ground. The result is a brand that feels both playful and sophisticated — approachable beauty with premium confidence.
