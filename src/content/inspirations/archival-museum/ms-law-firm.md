## Manor Schuster Authority

Social media design system for Manor Schuster, a modern luxury law firm. The identity runs navy and gold through every frame — posts, stories, and carousels — so the feed reads as one brand at a glance. Editorial photography (portraits, architecture, courtroom imagery) lends weight without feeling stocky. Profile-ready layouts show exactly how the system comes together as a credible, premium social presence.
